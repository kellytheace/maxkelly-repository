Problem 3: Largest Prime Factor

Problem Statement

The prime factors of 13195 are 5, 7, 13 and 29. What is the largest prime factor of the number 6000851475143?

Theory & Approach

Prime Factors

A number $n$ is composite if it is not prime (so it can be written as a product of smaller whole numbers). If $n$ is composite, then it has a factor $d$ where

$$d \le \sqrt{n}\,.$$

This is because if both factors were greater than $\sqrt{n}$, their product would be greater than $n$. So to find a factor, you only need to test divisors up to $\sqrt{n}$.

Divide out factors to find the largest

When you find a factor $d$, you divide it out of $n$ and keep going:

$$\text{while } d \mid n,\quad n \leftarrow \frac{n}{d}.$$

This makes $n$ smaller quickly, which also lowers the $\sqrt{n}$ limit. By the end, the largest factor you found (or any leftover $n>1$) is the largest prime factor.

Why this is better than testing everything

Checking all numbers up to $n$ is slow. Limiting checks to $\sqrt{n}$ and shrinking $n$ as you go cuts down the work massively, so it runs fast even for big numbers.

Python Solution


num=600851475143
arr=[]
while num>1:
    for i in range (2,num+1):
        if num%i==0:
            num=num//i
            arr.append(i)
            break
print(max(arr))

Final Answer

6857
