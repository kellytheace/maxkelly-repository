Problem 4: Largest Palindrome Product

Problem Statement

A palindromic number reads the same both ways. The largest palindrome made from the product of two $2$-digit numbers is $9009 = 91 \times 99$. Find the largest palindrome made from the product of two $3$-digit numbers.

Theory & Approach

To find the largest palindrome made from two $3$-digit numbers, we can just try every product $i \times j$ where $1 \le i \le 999$ and $1 \le j \le 999$. Whenever the product is a palindrome, we store it. After checking all pairs, the answer is simply the largest palindromic product we found, i.e. $\max(\text{arr})$. This works because we’ve tested every possible pair in the range.

Python Solution


def palindrome_check(n):
    return str(n) == str(n)[::-1]
arr=[]
for i in range(1,999+1):
    for j in range(1,999+1):
        x=i*j
        if palindrome_check(x)==True:
            arr.append(x)
print(max(arr))

Final Answer

906609
